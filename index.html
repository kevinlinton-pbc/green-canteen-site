    Why plant-based

    Less meat on the plate, less pressure on the planet

    Research by Elin Röös at the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences (SLU) looked at what happens when Swedes eat half as much meat, and a study by Bunge et al. looked at fully vegan diets. Switch the diet below and watch the footprint change.

    Good to know

    Questions

    Where can I buy a lunchbox?
    At the Uppsala Student Union, Övre Slottsgatan 7. Look for the Green Canteen counter.
    When are you open?
    Monday to Thursday, 09:00–16:00. Boxes are made in batches, so popular days can sell out before closing.
    How much does it cost?
    59 SEK, roughly €5, for a full lunch.
    Is everything vegan?
    Yes. Every dish is 100% plant-based: no meat, fish, dairy or eggs.
    Who cooks the food?
    Student volunteers from Plant-Based Campus Uppsala. Meals are prepared at Ulleråkergård and brought to the Student Union.
    Can my student union or nation work with you?
    We'd like that. We are looking for more partners, including at SLU Uppsala. Reach out through the Plant-Based Campus website.
